# Figure captions (numbers filled in by step5_figures.R)

**Figure 2.** Plasma L-kynurenine concentration and mean Hurst exponent of the somatomotor network in the 21 participants. Circles: migraine without aura (n = 16); triangles: migraine with aura (n = 5). The least-squares line and its 95% confidence band are shown as a visual guide; the statistic is the partial Spearman correlation adjusted for age and aura status (rho = 0.67, 95% BCa CI 0.38 to 0.87, permutation p = 0.0020; q = 0.014 across the seven networks, q = 0.14 across all 70 tests). Inset: the 21 leave-one-out estimates (range 0.61 to 0.72).

**Figure 3.** Partial Spearman correlations (adjusted for age and aura status) between the mean Hurst exponent of each of the seven networks and the nine plasma metabolites and the QUINA/KYNA ratio. Asterisks and bold type mark q < 0.05 after Benjamini-Hochberg correction within the family (L-KYN: 7 tests; all other measures: 63 tests); this was met for L-KYN with SMN, DAN, SVA, DMN. No test had q < 0.05 when all 70 tests were corrected together (smallest q = 0.14).

**Figure S1.** As Figure 2, restricted to the 16 participants without aura; partial Spearman correlation adjusted for age (rho = 0.61, 95% BCa CI 0.22 to 0.86, permutation p = 0.0175).
